Keep Signward free for everyone.

Signward is free, open source and local-first. No ads, no accounts, nothing sold. A few parts of it still cost real money to run, and that is what a donation covers.

Where your donation goes

  • The AI deep scan. The on-demand check that reads a page in plain language and explains why it looks safe or dangerous runs on a paid AI model.
  • The deeper checks. Brand and official-account lookups and domain-age checks that back up the local score use rate-limited paid data sources.
  • The server and mail. The API, the shared community blocklist and the feedback inbox all run on hosted infrastructure.
  • Building more. New chains, new drainer patterns and faster updates when a fresh scam shows up.

It is entirely voluntary. A donation never unlocks anything. Everyone gets the full tool, whether they give or not.
