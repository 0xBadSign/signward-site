Security incidents

When Signward flags a contract as compromised, this is where the details live: what happened, which approvals are affected, and how to revoke them. The alerts you see in the extension link here.

How this works

Signward keeps a short, human-reviewed list of contracts that have been exploited or are being used to drain wallets. If an approval you made is to one of these contracts, the extension warns you and points to the write-up here.

Each entry is verified against an official source before it is published. Nothing on this page can move your funds; the revoke action always happens in your own wallet, through Revoke.cash.
