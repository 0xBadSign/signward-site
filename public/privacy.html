Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Signward is a security tool, so it is built to see as little as possible. Almost everything happens on your device. This page explains exactly what stays local, the few checks that reach a server, and what is never collected.

In short

  • No account, no sign-up, no tracking, no ads, and nothing is sold.
  • Your keys and recovery phrase are never read, stored or transmitted.
  • Page scoring runs on your device. Full page URLs are never sent or logged.
  • A few optional checks send the minimum needed (an address, a domain, or a page snapshot you ask to scan).

1. On your device

These run entirely in your browser and send nothing:

  • Page checks. Signward scores each page locally against bundled lists and on-page signals to decide whether to warn or block.
  • The wallet gate. It reads and explains wallet requests (approvals, permits, orders, signatures) before you decide. It never holds a key and cannot sign for you.
  • Your approval ledger. Approvals you grant while Signward is installed are stored only in your browser's local storage, to power the revoke links and compromised-approval alerts. They are never uploaded.
  • Settings and caches. Your preferences, the downloaded lists, and per-tab scores are kept locally.

2. What is sent to a server, and only then

A small number of checks need Signward's backend (api.signward.app). Each sends the least it can, and only in the situation described:

CheckWhat is sentWhen
Address screeningA wallet/contract address and chain idOnline screening is on (default); can be turned off in settings
Domain ageA registrable domain (e.g. example.com) — never the full URLOnly for a page that already looks suspicious
Brand checkA short brand name taken from the page (e.g. “uniswap”) — never the URLOnly for a page that already looks suspicious
AI deep scanA page snapshot: the URL, title, a text sample, script and link hosts, and wallet-button labelsOnly when you click “Deep analysis”
Report a siteThe page URL you are reportingOnly when you click “Report this site”

List and feed updates (phishing lists, the community list, the compromised-contracts feed) are ordinary downloads and send no information about you. Transaction simulation is performed through your own wallet's network connection, not through Signward.

3. Third-party services

Some checks are answered with the help of established providers. Signward's server talks to them so that API keys stay off your device; they receive only the item above, never your identity:

  • GoPlus — malicious-address data for address screening.
  • Anthropic (Claude) — the AI model behind the on-demand deep scan.
  • Public domain records (RDAP, Certificate Transparency, and a WHOIS provider) — for the domain-age check.
  • An X (Twitter) data provider — to resolve a project's official account for the brand check.

4. What Signward never collects

  • Private keys, recovery phrases, or anything you type into a wallet.
  • Full page URLs or your browsing history.
  • Form inputs, passwords, or cookies.
  • Analytics, advertising identifiers, or behavioural tracking.

5. Retention and rate-limiting

Address, domain and brand answers are cached briefly on the server to reduce load, keyed by the item itself, not by you. Deep-scan snapshots are not stored. When you report a site, the reported URL enters a moderation queue; to prevent abuse, the server counts reports per client using a salted, truncated hash of the IP address — the raw IP address is never stored.

6. No sale of data

Signward does not sell, rent or share personal data. There is no advertising and no third-party analytics.

7. Open source

The extension, the backend and the lists are published so anyone can verify these claims. Secrets (API keys) are kept server-side and are not in the public code.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated and the current version is always available here.
