Signward for MetaMask

Signward's checks inside MetaMask's own confirmation screen: every transaction and signature request gets a plain-English explanation, and known drainer sites and addresses get a warning you must acknowledge before confirming.

Add Signward to MetaMask

Snaps run in the MetaMask browser extension on desktop.

What it can and cannot do

  • Can: explain every transaction and signature in plain English (approvals, Permit and Permit2, NFT approvals, EIP-7702 delegations, transfers).
  • Can: warn when the site is on Signward's community block list or the spender or recipient is a known drainer address.
  • Can: warn on unlimited approvals to unknown spenders.
  • Cannot: block a transaction on its own. MetaMask lets you decide after the warning.
  • Cannot: see wallet connections. The Signward browser extension covers that.
  • Cannot: protect Solana in MetaMask, which is not supported.

Permissions it asks for

  • Transaction insights. Shows the explanation and warnings on transaction confirmations.
  • Signature insights. Shows the explanation and warnings on signature requests.
  • Internet access. Only to download Signward's public block list and drainer address list from api.signward.app. Nothing about you or your transactions is sent.
  • Storage. To cache those lists.

Signward is not yet listed in the MetaMask Snaps Directory, so MetaMask shows a notice during installation that the Snap has not been verified by MetaMask. The source is open to inspection on npm.

For full protection (connect checks, site warnings, Solana), also add the browser extension for Chrome or Firefox.
